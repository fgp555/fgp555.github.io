Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Download the full version for 64-bit Windows and open the file. It includes everything the app needs to run.

Who is the lightweight version for?

It’s the same app in a smaller download for PCs that already have the 64-bit .NET 10 Desktop Runtime.

Where are my screenshots saved?

IMG Viewer saves them in Downloads. You can choose another folder in Settings.

Can I take a screenshot while using another app?

Yes. Leave IMG Viewer running next to the Windows clock and press Print Screen whenever you want to capture something.
